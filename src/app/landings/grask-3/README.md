# Grask landing page (Uncut Sans)

A copy of `src/app/landings/grask-2/` (the capital-G landing), made on 21 September 2026 from the committed state of that day, with one change: the type. Everything else (layout, copy, motion, colours other than the two text colours) is identical at the time of copying, so the two can be compared side by side at `/grask-2` and `/grask-3`. Edits to one do not affect the other.

## The type system

Spec: Uncut Sans as the primary face (headings, titles, buttons, navigation, labels, body) and Urbanist as the secondary (subtitles, descriptions, captions, helper text); no other family. Weights stop at 600. Two text colours. Hierarchy comes from size and those two colours, never from extra colours, underlines or caps.

- **Tokens** live in `grask-3.tokens.css` next to the palette: `--g-font-primary`, `--g-font-secondary`, `--g-text-primary` (#0D0D0D), `--g-text-secondary` (#83848B), `--g-weight-{display,title,ui,body}`, `--g-tracking-{display,title,ui,caption,body}`, `--g-leading-{display,title,caption,body}`. The kit's names still resolve (`--g-font-ui`, `--g-font-display` point at the primary face; `--g-text` at the primary colour; `--g-text-2` and `--g-text-3` both at the secondary), so nothing that reads them broke.
- **Roles** (the role table is in the tokens file):

  | Role | Where | Face and weight | Tracking | Leading |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | display | the `h1` | primary 600 | -0.05em | 1.08, `text-wrap: balance` |
  | title | `h2`, `h3`, `.display` (statement, quote, "Evidence report"), FAQ `summary` | primary 500 | -0.05em | 1.2 |
  | ui | buttons, nav, form labels, chips, LMS names, list items | primary 500 | -0.02em | as laid out |
  | caption | `.sub`: leads, card descriptions, helper text, small print | secondary 400 | -0.02em | 1.4 |
  | body | everything else: transcript quotes, the demo captions, the EU paragraph | primary 400 | -0.01em | 1.5 |

- **How they are applied.** `grask-3.css` sets body on the root and the display, title and `.sub` roles by selector. Sizes stay inline per element, as the canvas exported them; UI text sets `font-weight: var(--g-weight-ui); letter-spacing: var(--g-tracking-ui)` inline next to its size, the way it sets its colour. There is no numeric `font-weight` or `letter-spacing` left in the HTML. The -0.05em tracking only lands on text of 18px and up (the FAQ questions' minimum size went from 17.5px to 18px for that reason).
- **Fonts** are self-hosted in `fonts/` (SIL OFL, licence texts there): Uncut Sans 400/500/600 as static files (the family is not on Google Fonts; these are the latin files from `@fontsource/uncut-sans@5.3.0`) and Urbanist as a variable font (`@fontsource-variable/urbanist@5.3.0`), of which only 400 is used. Fallback stacks are the spec's: `"Uncut Sans", "Inter", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` and `"Urbanist", "Outfit", system-ui, sans-serif`.
- **Font smoothing:** `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased` and `-moz-osx-font-smoothing: grayscale` on the root.
- **Not applied from the spec:** the page background stays the kit's paper (#FAFAF7) rather than #FFFFFF, and cards stay white, because the card system relies on white-on-paper; the spec's light surface (#F3F5F6) is `--g-surface-2`, the LMS tiles. Flip `--g-paper` to try the white page. The hero headline is larger than the spec's 2.5 : 1.4 : 1 scale (80px against a 21px lead); section titles are on it (54 : 26 : 19.5).
- **Judgement calls:** the demo captions (the spoken lines) are body, not title, so they read as speech; the "No" badges and the two lockups use the display weight (600) and tracking; the giant footer wordmark is re-derived for the new face (see the `.giant` comment in `grask-3.css`).

What differs from `grask/` (inherited from `grask-2/`):

- Every mention of the name in the copy is `Grask` (the domain `grask.eu` and the "ask + grasp" line are unchanged).
- The kit's lockup and wordmark SVGs are outlined paths that spell "grask", so they are not used. The header and footer show the mark tile (`public/landings/grask-3/logo/grask-mark-tile.svg`) next to the name as live text in the display font (`.lockup`), and the giant footer wordmark is the name as text, edge to edge like Instructure's footer: sized in container units (`39.6cqw`, see the comment on `.giant` in `grask-3.css`) so it always fills the viewport width, with its bottom cropped by the page end.
- LMS section: four logos only (Moodle, Blackboard, Canvas, Brightspace) on quiet light-grey tiles (`--g-surface-2`), full colour at every position, no shadows or dimming, in the style of an Apple-like integrations panel; the loop is one continuous glide (since 2026-09-22) and starts with Moodle at the centre. See **LMS arc** below.
- Bands (grask-3 only, 2026-09-21): every plate whose colour differs from the page, the tinted demo (`#how`), the white LMS plate (`#lms`), the ink "students and data" plate (`#trust`) and the petrol sandbox (`#sandbox`), is a full-bleed band, edge to edge, instead of a rounded card on the 1200px column. Each section has no max-width or side padding and its plate no radius; the plate pads horizontally by `--glp3-col` (the column's centring margin plus the page gutter, defined on the root in `grask-3.css`) plus its own old padding, so the content sits exactly where it did. The plates' children are not wrapped in a column div (a leftover of the pause checkboxes' `~` sibling selectors, removed on 2026-09-22). In the LMS band the title keeps a gutter and the bottom bar (`.plate-bar`) is inset the same way inline; the orbit clips at the viewport edges.
- Section order (grask-3 only, 2026-09-22): the statement (`#why`) is the second section, straight after the hero, so the page states the problem before it shows the demo: hero, `#why`, `#how` (the demo), `#outcomes` (the outcome rail, since 2026-09-25; until then `#where`, the three "where it fits" cards), `#lms`, `#trust`, `#faq`, `#sandbox`, footer. The steps rail (`#steps`, "From rubric to grade": four cards travelling horizontally while pinned, with a progress bar) was removed the same day. The demo plate, no longer on the opening screen, is a scroll reveal (`data-reveal`) instead of part of the hero entrance (`.rise-5`, which the pilot page still uses).
- Scope class `.grask-3-lp`, keyframe/utility prefix `glp3-`, assets under `public/landings/grask-3/`, so nothing collides with `grask/` or `grask-2/` when they are loaded together.

The rest of this file is the original's, with those names substituted.

## How it is put together

- **No leaks.** The component uses `ViewEncapsulation.None`, but every selector is scoped under `.grask-3-lp` and every keyframe is prefixed `glp3-`, so nothing touches the rest of your app.
- **Small stylesheets, split by job.** `grask.tokens.css`, `grask.fonts.css`, `grask.css` (base), `demo`, `demo-timeline`, `strip`, `orbit`, `motion`, `scroll`, `rail`, `glass`.
- **Brand kit (applied 2026-09-20; fonts and text colours replaced 2026-09-21, see above).** Colours, radii and logos come from `bffyb/common/styling` (source of truth: its `tokens.json`). `grask.tokens.css` is a copy of the kit's `tokens.css`, scoped to `.grask-3-lp`, plus a few landing-only derived aliases (`--g-border-input`, `--g-ink-rgb`, `--g-on-accent-2`, `--g-text-faint`, `--g-tint-strong-rgb`) for things the kit has no token for yet. Every inline style references a `--g-*` alias, never a raw hex, so a palette change is a one-file edit. Logos are the kit's SVGs under `public/landings/grask-3/logo/` (lockup in header and footer, mark tile in the hero, wordmark in the footer). 
- **Corner radii** (one scale since 2026-09-21, grask-3 only; tokens at the end of `grask-3.tokens.css`): every radius on the page is `--g-radius-button` (16px, added later on 2026-09-21: the CTA buttons, since 2026-09-23 three of them, "Book a demo" ×2 and "Open the sandbox"; "Try it out" and "Watch a check run" are text links since then), `--g-radius-control` (8px: "End call", the form inputs, the grade field, the strip's segment buttons, and the 56–60px icon tiles), `--g-radius-card` (16px: the demo cards, the outcome rail's cards, the pilot panel, the FAQ rows, the "No" panels, the recording strip) or `--g-radius-pill` (9999px: tags and badges, and thin bars such as the voice bars, waveform bars, playhead, skeleton lines, progress bar and scrollbar thumb, where full rounding is what the eye sees anyway). Nested panels follow outer radius minus the padding between them, minimum 8px; every such gap here is 20px or more, so nested elements are simply the control value. Left as they are: the circles (recording dot, FAQ plus), the hero marker (square), the demo evidence highlight `.hl` (`--g-radius-sm`, a text highlight) and the LMS logo tiles (`.orbit-tile`, a radius proportional to the tile size). The button sweep's `::before` inherits the radius, and focus rings follow it.
- **Self-hosted fonts.** See **The type system** above: Uncut Sans and Urbanist replace the kit's Schibsted Grotesk and Instrument Sans on this copy. Local files in `fonts/`, so the page makes no third-party requests.
- **In-page links** go through `go($event)`, which scrolls with `scrollIntoView`. They work under any route and any `<base href>`.
- **Page motion** (added 2026-09-20, see `grask.motion.css`): scroll reveals on `data-reveal` elements (staggered with `data-delay="1".."6"`; `data-reveal="mask"` rises out of a clipped parent), a hero band of floating icon tiles (removed 2026-09-21 with the recording strip, see below), scroll-driven parallax (`.drift`, `--dy`; unused since the "Where it fits" cards became the spread on 2026-09-21), hover lift on cards (`.lift`; unused since the outcome rail replaced those cards on 2026-09-25), and, until 2026-09-21, a nav shadow after 140px of scroll (replaced by the header states above). The only JavaScript is an IntersectionObserver in `grask.ts` that adds `.glp3-js` to the root and toggles `.is-in` per element; without it nothing is hidden. Reveals replay: an element that drops back out below the viewport snaps hidden again and re-enters on the next scroll down (elements that leave through the top stay shown; flip `mirror` in `setupReveals()` to replay in both directions). Property ownership: reveal owns `transform`, drift owns `translate`, lift owns `scale`.
- **Header** (straight bar since 2026-09-21, grask-3 only): a full-width sticky bar on the 1200px column, `--glp3-header-h` (72px) tall: lockup left, links centred (a `1fr auto 1fr` grid, so the nav is centred on the page and not between its neighbours; the buttons are pinned to column 3 so they stay right when the links hide under 920px), and on the right "Try it out" (a text link in the nav's style, `.bar-link`, opens the sandbox URL in a new tab; added 2026-09-23 as an outlined button, demoted to a link the same day) next to "Book a demo", outlined: the hero's filled "Book a demo" is the page's one primary action (since 2026-09-23; before that the header's was filled too and the hero had a second, outlined button). The outlined button has a 1.5px border in the text colour (since 2026-09-23; before that a 14% ink hairline). It hides on the way down and returns on the way up (`setupHeaderHide()` in `grask-3.ts`, states on `.bar` in `grask-3.css`): within 80px of the top it is shown, transparent and borderless (`.is-top`; `scrollY <= 0` counts as the top, so iOS overscroll never hides it); beyond that it is solid paper at 95% with a 1px hairline (`rgba(ink, .08)`); it hides (`.is-hidden`, `translateY(-100%)`, 200ms ease, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`; a transform, so nothing reflows) only once the hero's content is completely above the viewport (its last element, the recording strip; since 2026-09-23, when the hero stopped being centred: the section is a full screen tall and its lower part is empty paper, so measuring the section kept the bar on an empty screen) and after 10px of downward travel since the scroll last changed direction, and 10px upward brings it back. It never hides while something in it has keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`; `focusin` also forces it shown) or while a toggle in it has `aria-expanded="true"` (there is no mobile menu yet; that is for when one is added). The scroll listener is passive and coalesced to one update per frame. Without JS it is simply the solid bar. Every section a nav link points to (`#top`, `#how`, `#lms`, `#trust`, `#faq`, `#sandbox`) measures the header through `--glp3-header-h` (`scroll-margin-top`). The pill and its liquid glass (below) are no longer used by the header.
- **Buttons** (wisprflow.ai hover since 2026-09-21, grask-3 only; CSS next to `.btn-dark` in `grask-3.css`, JS in `setupButtonWave()` in `grask-3.ts`): on hover the button presses in (`scale(.98)`, 200ms ease) and its label ripples letter by letter. Every button label is a `<span>`, which the JS splits into one `.word` per word and one `.char` per letter (both inline-block, so letters can move and words never break); on `mouseenter` each letter runs the same 1s linear ripple, 60ms after the previous one (up 20% of its height tilted -5°, down 20% tilted +5°, back to rest; Web Animations API, no library), and `mouseleave` cancels the rest and eases every letter home in 200ms. Pointer devices only (`hover: hover`); under `prefers-reduced-motion` neither the press nor the ripple runs; without JS only the press remains. Same numbers as the original (GSAP SplitText + a timeline of `yPercent: -20/20/0`, `rotation: -5/5/0`, `i * 0.06` stagger). Letters are separate boxes, so kerning pairs inside a label are lost (as on the original). The three header nav links (`.nav-links a`), the header's "Try it out" (`.bar-link`) and the strip's "Watch a check run" title (`.strip-cta`) get the same ripple and keep their petrol hover colour, but not the press (as on the original); their underline hover is off (`.nav-links .plain:hover`, `.bar-link:hover`, `.strip-cta:hover`), since a decoration never reaches into inline-block letters and would only show under the spaces. Footer links are untouched.
- **Pilot page** (since 2026-09-21, grask-3 only): the pilot application ("Apply for a free formative pilot" and the form) is no longer a section of the landing but its own page at `/grask-3/pilot`, `pilot/pilot.{ts,html}` in this folder, registered under `pages` on the grask-3 entry in `registry.ts` (`app.routes.ts` routes a landing's pages at `/<slug>/<path>`; they are not in the gallery or the switcher, which read the landing from the first URL segment, so the device stage and `[` `]` still work there). The two "Book a demo" buttons (header, outlined; hero, filled) and the Pilot footer link are `routerLink="/grask-3/pilot"` (the Pilot and "Students and data" nav links were removed on 2026-09-22). The page: the same section markup, centred on a `100dvh` page under a static, transparent header (lockup and a "Back to Grask" link, both router links to `/grask-3`); the two columns enter with the hero's `.rise` animation instead of the scroll reveals (no observer on that page); same tokens, fonts and `grask-3.css`, the page scrollbar and the button hover (`grask-3.wave.ts`, which both pages import). It scrolls to the top on entry, since the router keeps the window's scroll position between routes.
- **Liquid glass** (added 2026-09-20, see `grask.glass.css`; not used by the header since 2026-09-21, kept for any other element): the former header pill was a port of React Bits' [GlassSurface](https://reactbits.dev/components/glass-surface) (MIT). Its `backdrop-filter` is an inline SVG filter: an `feImage` displacement map the size of the pill (edges are red/blue gradients, the centre is mid-grey, so the backdrop bends only at the rim) drives three `feDisplacementMap`s at slightly different scales, one per colour channel, for the chromatic fringe. `setupLiquidGlass()` in `grask.ts` builds the filter for every `[data-liquid-glass]` element, sizes the map to it and regenerates it on resize; the lens shape is the `LIQUID_GLASS` constant there, the by-eye tunables (`--glp3-glass-frost`, `--glp3-glass-blur`, `--glp3-glass-saturation`) are at the top of `grask.glass.css`. Chrome and Edge only, as in React Bits: Safari renders `url()` backdrop filters wrong and Firefox rejects them, so there (and without JS, and under `prefers-reduced-transparency`) the pill is the plain frosted blur. To glass another element, give it `class="glass" data-liquid-glass` and a `border-radius`.
- **Opening screen** (added 2026-09-20, modelled on nomic.ai; the recording strip replaced the icon band on 2026-09-21): the hero (`.hero`) sits under the header bar (in flow above it) with a fixed top padding of `clamp(64px, 7.8vw, 112px)`, its content anchored to the top (`justify-content: flex-start`) and no minimum height, so the statement track (`#why`) starts right after the recording strip plus the hero's bottom padding (since 2026-09-23; until then the hero was `min-height: calc(100dvh - var(--glp3-header-h))` with its content centred, so the statement started exactly at the fold and, once the content was anchored to the top, an empty screen sat between the strip and the statement; the round scroll cue that sat on the hero's bottom edge was removed on 2026-09-22). In order: the headline ("Every student — heard. Without the hours.", two lines, the highlighter `.mark` under the last two words only; until 2026-09-23 "Without dozens of hours spent." with the whole second line highlighted), the lead (`--g-text-lead`, #444, weight 400; two lines at 820px: what it runs and what it hands back; the dashboard sentence moved to the demo section, see below), one filled "Book a demo" (the page's primary action; the outlined "Watch a check run" button became the strip's title), and the **recording strip** (below), the hero's visual. The title is split into one `<span class="w">` per word (joined with `&ngsp;`, since Angular strips whitespace-only text nodes between elements) and the words drop in from 30px above, 80ms apart (`glp3-word`, from 0.2s, the sixth word lands at 1.2s); the highlighter, copy, button and strip follow (`.mark-in` 1.1s, `.rise-3` 0.9s, `.rise-4` 1.05s, `.rise-6` 1.3s). Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the words are simply there.
- **Recording strip** (added 2026-09-21, the hero's visual since 2026-09-23, see `grask-3.strip.css`): one white card under the hero button (max 1080px, 22–28px padding, 16px radius, a 12% ink border and a soft shadow, ~197px tall; until 2026-09-23 960px, 20px, a 10% hairline, no shadow, ~136px): a head row with the card's title on the left, "Watch a check run", a petrol play disc and an in-page link to `#how` (`.strip-cta`, the former hero button), and on the right the context ("Lab 3 · hash tables · oral check") and a live clock, "02:14 / 06:12" in tabular figures; a waveform of 120 bars (4px, rounded, pale blue, 56px tall; heights are the fixed `STRIP_BARS` array in `grask-3.ts`, a seeded LCG, so nothing re-randomises), three thin dividers, and four labels, one per rubric criterion with its time range (`STRIP_SEGMENTS`; widths and divider positions are inline from the segment seconds over 372). The 2px teal playhead with a knob and the dark-teal "played" bars (a second row of bars clipped by an animated inset) run on the demo's 34s clock: the sweep takes 0–92.7% of the loop (until the demo's "Call ended" caption), then the strip holds fully played with all four checks until the loop restarts. The clock's elapsed seconds are a registered `<integer>` custom property (`--glp3-t`, 0 to 372 over the sweep) rendered by two CSS counters, minutes `round(down, t / 60)` and seconds `mod(t, 60)`, in a `::before`; browsers without math functions in `counter-reset` (`@supports`) show the total only. A label is petrol while the playhead is inside its segment; when it leaves, the label turns ink and its check pops in, opacity and scale driven by `--glp3-done`, a registered `<number>` that ramps 0 → 1 over ~0.33s in the `glp3-seg1..4` keyframes (both `@property` names are `glp3-`-prefixed because `@property` is document-global). The check's negative right margin cancels its width for line breaking, so it hangs into the label's 18px right padding instead of wrapping alone onto an empty line when a name just fits. The labels are buttons: `seek(i)` moves every animation in the demo stage and the strip, including the clock, to that criterion's start (Web Animations API `currentTime`), and `syncStrip()` aligns the strip to the demo's first caption at start. Reduced motion: no playhead, no clock, strip shown fully played. Under 720px 48 of the bars show (the others are `display: none`; the flex spacing re-spreads the rest), the wave is 44px, the labels lose their times and the head loses the context. A visually hidden sentence (`.vh`) describes the strip for screen readers. Verified on 2026-09-23 in Chrome 153 and Firefox 157 (headless): both tick the clock and pop the checks.
- **Dashboard line** (2026-09-23): the lead's last sentence ("Bundled with a dashboard that helps you find and fix weak spots in the study programme") left the hero for the bottom of the demo plate (`#how`): a `.plate-foot` row after the stage, a chip in the plate-bar's style ("Across the cohort") and one sentence, revealed on scroll like the plate.
- **Page scrollbar** (added 2026-09-20): the document scrollbar is styled to the design while this landing is on screen: a slim rounded thumb in muted petrol on the paper track, no rail. It lives on `<html>`, outside the component, so `grask-3.ts` adds `glp3-page` to `<html>` after first render and removes it on destroy; `grask-3.css` styles `html.glp3-page::-webkit-scrollbar*` (Chromium/WebKit) and, under `@supports not selector(::-webkit-scrollbar)`, `scrollbar-width`/`scrollbar-color` (Firefox). The tokens file also scopes to `html.glp3-page` so the rules use `--g-*`. Other routes keep the browser default.
- **Section scrolling** (added 2026-09-21, see `grask-3.scroll.css`): the page scrolls freely (a snap jump from the hero to the second section, added 2026-09-21, was dropped on 2026-09-22; the only scripted scrolling left is the in-page links' `go()`), and two sections turn the scroll into something else, the statement and the outcome rail (its own bullet below): `#why` (since 2026-09-22) is pinned for 100vh of scroll (`.stop` is the 200dvh track, `.stop-pin` the sticky screen) while its two sentences appear out of an empty screen, centred a little above its middle (the pin's padding is the header height on top and 20dvh below, so the content box's centre sits at about 44% of the viewport; since 2026-09-23, before that 78px and 112px, the geometric centre; anchoring the text to the hero's top line was tried the same day and reverted, it left the lower two thirds of the screen empty), letter by letter in reading order (`.fill`, a `background-clip: text` wipe from transparent to ink with a 120px soft edge (`--edge` on `.fill`; the background is 200% plus the edge wide so both end states stay clean) on the track's view timeline; the unfilled text was faint grey until 2026-09-22 and is invisible since: nothing shows until the section's top reaches the top of the viewport, `contain 0%`, then the first sentence fills over 4–46%, the second over 46–88%, strictly after it, and the finished statement holds for the last 12vh before the demo scrolls up; in browsers without scroll-driven animations, Firefox as of 2026-09-22, `setupStatementFill()` in `grask-3.ts` writes the same progress to `--glp3-why-p` on the track from a passive scroll listener and the stylesheet maps it onto the same ranges, so the effect is identical there). The statement pins and fills at every width in every browser and only needs no `prefers-reduced-motion`; otherwise it lays out as one plain screen with its text in ink. (Until 2026-09-25 `#where` pinned here too, for 80vh while its three cards fanned out of a stack into their row, `animation-timeline` only and 960px and up; the outcome rail replaced it.)
- **Outcome rail** (added 2026-09-25, see `grask-3.rail.css` + `grask-3.rail.ts`): the section after the demo, `#outcomes`, in place of the three "Where it fits first" cards, ported from `src/app/landings/grask-rail/` (its README has the mechanics, worked out from the outcomes section on getfluently.app). An ink band pins to the screen for 135vh of scroll while six outcome cards (a live check, the student's quote, the ticked rubric, the cohort chart, the team row, the LMS assignment row) slide sideways in the 1120px column, the track chasing the scroll target on an overdamped spring (k 500, c 60, m 1; `setupRail()`, a scroll listener and rAF, so Firefox gets the same motion), and each card's widget springs up once as its card comes into view (an IntersectionObserver plus a CSS `linear()` spring; the stage clips the track, so a card still off to the right does not count as in view). Under 720px there is no runway: the cards run as a marquee over a second copy of the list; under `prefers-reduced-motion` the track follows the scroll directly, the widgets fade in place and the marquee becomes a swipeable, snapping row. Fitted to this page: full bleed like the other bands (no radius), the eyebrow as a chip in the trust band's style, the h2 and the card titles in the title role, the widget labels in sentence case, the radius scale (cards `--g-radius-card`, the glass widgets `--g-radius-control` as nested panels, tags and chips pill), the fonts, tint and on-dark text from the tokens; the widgets' live / ok / warn colours and the avatar colours are their own. The classes that would meet the strip's `.wave`, the demo's `.stage` (both targets of `seek()`) or the header's `.glass` are prefixed `rail-`; keyframes are `glp3-rail-*`. The card data (titles, tones, delays; the names and numbers on the widgets are made up) is `RAIL` in `grask-3.rail.ts`. Checked on 2026-09-25 in headless Chrome at 1600 × 900, 1280 × 680, 820 × 1180 and 390 × 844 and in headless Firefox 157 at 1600 × 900.
- **Motion is CSS only,** apart from the rail's scrub above and the statement's fill where there is no `animation-timeline`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` both loops stop on a complete, readable frame. (The Pause/Play switches on the demo and the LMS band, each a visually hidden checkbox plus a sibling selector, were removed on 2026-09-22.)

- **Demo timeline:** one 34 s loop. Every percentage is a moment in it (1 s = 2.94%). Captions (`cap0` to `cap5`), word-by-word reveal (`say0` to `say4`), evidence rows and highlights (`ev1` to `ev3`, `hl1` to `hl3`), the grade ring (`grade`) and the voice bars (`voice`) all share that clock, so they stay in sync, and `seek()` moves them together.
- **LMS arc:** one keyframe path through five positions, `--glp3-p0` to `--glp3-p4` (`p2` is the centre; `p0`/`p4` are the hidden enter/exit). Four logos run the same 14 s path (4 × 3.5 s) as one continuous glide (since 2026-09-22; before that each quarter was a 1.1 s slide and a 2.4 s hold): linear timing, a waypoint every 12.5% with the midpoints on the same arc so there are no kinks, opacity only fading at the hidden ends. A tile passes the centre every 3.5 s and its label crossfades in while the tile is within about 1.5 units of it (37.5–62.5% of the loop). Markup order is the order they reach the centre, and the delays put tile i at 50% − 25% × (i − 1) of the loop on load, so the first (Moodle) is at the centre and the next arrives 3.5 s later. `--u` is the size unit and shrinks at three breakpoints. To change the number of logos, the loop length, the delays and the keyframe percentages change together.
- **Layout** is inline styles, as exported from the canvas; colours in them are `--g-*` tokens. Mapping from the export: page → paper, black buttons → petrol, the blue "student voice" → petrol, the yellow highlighter → tint-strong, card tints → tint / tint-strong / petrol, the dark plate → ink. Status colours (demonstrated / partial / missing) are reserved for a real evidence report and are not used on this page.
