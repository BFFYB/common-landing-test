How it moves

  1. A pinned stage. The dark section is 235vh tall: a sticky stage one screen high, then a 135vh spacer. The stage stays put while the spacer scrolls past.
  2. Scroll becomes sideways travel. Over that 135vh the card track is scrubbed from its first card flush with the 1200px column's left edge to its last card flush with the right edge. Scroll back and it runs back.
  3. On a spring, not glued to the wheel. The track chases that target on an overdamped spring (stiffness 500, damping 60), so a flick glides and a stop settles without bounce.
  4. Widgets rise once. Each card's widget waits 80px low and transparent. When it comes into view (the stage clips cards still off to the right) it rises on a bouncy spring, a little staggered from its neighbours. Hover one to nudge it.
  5. Phones get a marquee. Under 720px there is no runway: the cards run leftwards at 75px a second, looping over a second copy.

Under reduced motion the track follows the scroll directly, the widgets fade in without moving and the phone marquee becomes a swipeable row. The motion follows the outcomes section on getfluently.app; the copy is Grask's, and the names and numbers on the cards are made up.
