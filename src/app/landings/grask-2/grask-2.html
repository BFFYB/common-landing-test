Questions

Does the AI grade?

No. Grask conducts the conversation and organises what was said by rubric criterion. You review the evidence and you grade. The AI produces evidence, not verdicts.

How much of my time does it take?

Conducting the conversations takes none of your time. Reviewing the evidence and grading does, and that part stays with you.

Is this a proctoring tool?

No. Grask does not monitor students, infer emotion or stress, or use voice biometrics. It holds a conversation with a student about their own work.

Which languages does it work in?

English today. German, Dutch, French, Italian, Spanish and Polish are planned.

Do I need approval from my study board?

Formative checks carry no grade, so they usually sit within the instructor’s own remit. Your institution’s rules decide.

What does a pilot cost?

The formative pilots planned for December 2026 are free.
