How it moves

  • The section is a tall runway; a full-height stage inside it is position: sticky.
  • Cards are absolute at the stage centre and spaced along a track: card j sits at offset xj, gap = 14% of the median card width.
  • Scroll progress moves a single head from 0 to the last offset. Each card's screen x is head − xj.
  • That x also sets rotateX, −50° to +50° across ±2.1 card widths, eased power1.inOut, with transform-origin: 50% 50% −orbit (orbit = 65% of a card width) so the card swings on an arc instead of spinning in place.
  • The stage has perspective = 2.6 card widths; x is divided by the resulting shrink so spacing reads even on screen. z-index follows distance from centre.
  • The head follows the scroll through an expo-out of 1.5 s (GSAP's scrub: 1.5); on phones it is direct. The section's corners are a second scroll-scrubbed value.
